# Security Policy



## Reporting a Vulnerability



## accepting bugs are (using demo accounts or own accounts) 

* **XSS**
* **sqli**
* **0 click account hijacking**
* **session hijacking**
* **low hanging fruits**
* **csrf**
* **ssrf**

with POC


## not allowed attacks : 

* **dos**
* **attacks involving the real clients accounts**
